Rolex Submariner Ref. 6538 "Big Crown"

Originally sold in 1962 for approximately $150. Today, pristine vintage 6538 models command between $200,000 and $500,000+ at global horological auctions due to their association with Sean Connery's early films.

Breitling Top Time Ref. 2002 (Geiger Counter)

The original screen-used prop from 1965's Thunderball was discovered at an English car boot sale in 2013 for £25. It subsequently fetched over £103,800 ($160,000+) at Sotheby's.

Seiko Digital Resurgence

Mass-market digital references from the late 1970s and 80s (e.g., Seiko 0674-5009 and G757-5020) experienced renewed collector interest, transforming vintage quartz modules into sought-after luxury collectibles.
